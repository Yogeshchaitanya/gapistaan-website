HomeOne Year Diploma in Camera Acting

Course Briefing

The One Year Diploma in Camera Acting is designed for aspiring actors who want to master on-camera performance. This program provides comprehensive training in acting techniques, dialogue delivery, emotions, expressions, and body language for both cinema and television.

Course Highlights

  • Professional Acting Techniques for Film & TV
  • Voice Modulation and Diction Training
  • Body Language, Expression & Improvisation
  • On-Camera Workshops & Scene Practice
  • Script Reading & Character Development
  • Audition Training and Portfolio Shoot

Duration

1 Year (Full-Time Program)

Eligibility

Minimum Age: 16 years
No prior acting experience required.

Career Opportunities

After completion, students can pursue careers in Films, TV Serials, Web Series, Advertisements, and Theatre.
