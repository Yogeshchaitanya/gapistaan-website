Someone has used Chacha's soap in the hostel room, and worse, cut it in two. That one bar of soap turns the whole room into a courtroom. Chacha questions the boys one by one, sniffs their hands for evidence, and the Gen Z boys swear they use shampoo, not soap.

Along the way the argument spills over to a ₹7200 pair of glasses, an FX3 camera and "hygiene". The boys have turned the room into Film City, where a rehearsal of king, minister and soldier is going on at 9 at night, and Chacha announces that the moment he finds another hostel, he is leaving. In the end the soap thief's secret comes out.

This short 6-minute episode is fully improvised: no script, no fixed dialogue, just a situation and cameras that keep rolling.
