The class at the acting institute begins with an exercise, the "I am not the police, you are Basanti" game, which soon turns into "you are a fool". Then the real argument begins: does the future belong to cinema or to YouTube?

One side argues that 300 people stand in line in the casting corridors and casting directors pick their own people, so open a YouTube channel right away and build a body. The other side asks: can you work as hard as Shah Rukh Khan? In between come YouTubers earning crores, six-pack abs, Salman bhai's fans and Chacha's slap-happy temper.

This is Gapistaan's first episode, where the fight of Chacha versus Gen Z began.
