At the academy, Chacha is rehearsing a line, "I admit, there are some things I am not good at", but even after 50 takes he cannot remember the next line. The Gen Z boys do not miss the chance and keep taking digs at his age and memory. Then a debate starts: do Chacha's curly locks look like Mithun's, Rajinikanth's or Akshay Kumar's?

Then a new girl arrives at the academy for a crash course, and the whole mood of the room changes. One claims to have notes on the Natyashastra, another offers his FX3 camera, and everyone sets out to prove he is the greatest actor. But as she leaves, the boys call out "Chacha" to him in front of everyone, and all of Chacha's image is gone in an instant.

How a girl walking in changes the boys' behaviour is the comedy of this improvised episode.
