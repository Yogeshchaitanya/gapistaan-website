Chacha is about to get the first audition of his life, and the role is a transgender character. He is fully prepared: sari, lipstick, gajra and bindi. The Gen Z boys cannot stop laughing, but Chacha makes it clear that no role is to be mocked, this one or any other, and every role deserves respect.

Then there is a knock on the door and Ayushi walks in. To keep the secret safe, the boys introduce Chacha, sitting there in the sari, as "Chacha's Chachi". Now Chacha's faults are discussed right in front of "Chachi", and then a heated argument breaks out about protests and politics. In the end the veil is lifted and the whole secret is out.

Audition, blessings and protest: an episode that is completely improvised.
